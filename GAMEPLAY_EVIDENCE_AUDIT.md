## Editorial rules

1. **Gameplay-verified** means the actual gameplay footage was viewed and the specific instruction, starting point and result were observed, or an authorized tester followed the route and recorded evidence.
2. **Video-indexed** means the publisher's chapter list names an item and a timestamp, without visual frame review.
3. **Source-supported** means a written guide supports the claim, but conflicting accounts and patch differences may exist.
4. Never imply that a timestamp confirms a quest deadline if it only shows an item pickup.
5. Prioritize cutoff warnings and prerequisite dialogue over generic collectible descriptions. Record uncertainty rather than guessing.
