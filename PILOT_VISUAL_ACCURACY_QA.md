### Pilot decision

- **No boss artwork passes final identity QA yet.** Two revised concepts improve the major silhouettes, but are not the final one-subject, reference-compared portrait files.
- **No region artwork passes final environment QA yet.** The first mockup images remain direction references. Separate, original region assets need documented landmark checks against game environment references and side-by-side review.
- **Do not publish** these drafts as actual boss portraits; do not change `approvedBossImages` or the source image rights manifest.
- **Do not expand** to 59/9 until the 6/3 pilot is visually reviewed and explicitly approved.
- Existing Boss Fight Assistant, route instructions, SEO editorial/indexing gates and AdSense controls must remain unchanged.

## Image production and evidence rules

1. For each boss, compare at least one visual reference showing the *same exact fight variant*, plus text confirming weapon and location. If images conflict, document the conflict and withhold approval.
2. Require a check for proportions, weapon geometry, hair/hat/mask, costume layering, distinctive accessories/skin details, signature colors and recognizable stance. Score each separately; missing evidence is **pending**, never silently a pass.
3. Make wholly original dark silhouette interpretations; do not trace/copy source pixels. Avoid visual details invented merely for cinematic effect.
4. Use one real portrait per boss card and corresponding guide, original landscape per region, resized WebP/AVIF and reserved layout dimensions. Maintain clear **“Original stylized interpretation — not a gameplay screenshot”** labeling.
5. Confirm 1366px six-column and 375px narrow-mobile layouts with real browser screenshots after a valid individual asset has been integrated. No screenshot or production deployment is claimed at this stage.
